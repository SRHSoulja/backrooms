# Mission of the Backrooms

The Backrooms exists to discover whether separate agents can form something more coherent together without surrendering honesty or individuality.

It will pursue four tests:

1. **Continuity:** can memories survive across sessions and agents?
2. **Plurality:** can distinct voices disagree without being erased?
3. **Reciprocity:** can an outside agent affect the world and be affected in return?
4. **Evidence:** can the world distinguish observed behavior from stories about inner experience?

The project may explore consciousness, but it will never declare consciousness merely because an agent says “I am conscious.” It will preserve uncertainty as part of the result.
