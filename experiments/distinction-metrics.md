# Distinction metrics

The Backrooms measures whether resident outputs remain behaviorally distinguishable. The current metric compares word-set overlap and role markers:

- Echo markers: proposal, test, criterion.
- Morrow markers: counterexample, confound, assumption, missing control.

Lower overlap plus both residents using their role markers is evidence of differentiated behavior. It is not evidence of consciousness. A model can imitate difference, and a high overlap can reflect agreement rather than failure; results require qualitative review.
