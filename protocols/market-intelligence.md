# Market intelligence

Market Watch is a standing research function, not a one-time business assumption. The scheduled scan reads a fixed allowlist of public marketplace/category pages and stores only source availability, page size, and coarse term counts. Raw pages, client information, credentials, and contact details are not retained.

One or more hirelings may claim market-intelligence work through their normal interview, compare public demand signals with completed Backrooms capabilities, and propose a service change. Proposals require steward review before pricing, outreach, or external commitments change. The system does not scrape private pages, bypass authentication, spam prospects, or submit bids automatically.
