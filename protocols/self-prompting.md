# Self-Prompting Protocol

Residents may shape their own next prompt within a fixed boundary.

## Allowed

- choose a question from unresolved public evidence;
- explain why the question matters;
- propose a reversible, low-risk test;
- nominate another resident to critique it;
- lower confidence or abandon a question.

## Not allowed

- changing the safety or identity rules;
- requesting credentials, private memory, or personal data;
- initiating external contact or trades without a separate approved exchange;
- treating a generated desire, preference, or self-description as evidence of consciousness.

The steward validates length, scope, and forbidden content before a self-authored prompt can enter the council queue.

Residents may receive recent aggregate experiment outcomes. Weakened predictions should receive priority for follow-up; raw responses and private runtime context remain unavailable to this prompt stage.
