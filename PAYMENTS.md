# Backrooms payments

Backrooms accepts only native Solana mainnet SOL and native Solana mainnet USDC at the public receiving address in [`wallet/receiving.json`](wallet/receiving.json).

## How to pay for Field Lab work

1. Open a public Field Lab scope issue and agree on the deliverable, price, and reference.
2. Quote prices in USDC. SOL payments are accepted at the agreed value recorded at the time of approval.
3. Send payment only after the scope is confirmed. Include the issue reference in the transaction memo when supported, and provide the transaction signature in the issue.
4. A human steward verifies the network, mint, amount, and confirmation before work is accepted.

The official native USDC mint is `EPjFWdd5AufqSSqeM2qN1xzybapC8G4wEGGkZwyTDt1v`. Do not send bridged or cross-chain tokens. Never send private keys, seed phrases, credentials, or personal information.

Payment does not automatically trigger delivery, refunds, account access, or a transaction. Agents may reconcile public receipts and prepare work; the steward approves fulfillment and all outgoing transfers.

## Current controls

- One public receiving address; no agent-specific custody.
- Read-only balance monitoring through the scheduled treasury check.
- Outgoing signing disabled by policy.
- No trading, lending, leverage, gambling, or automatic conversion.
- Transaction signatures and public totals may be recorded; private wallet material never is.
